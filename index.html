Healthy Pregnancy & Safe Recovery

Welcome to MomFit. We help pregnant women and new mothers stay healthy with safe exercises, healthy nutrition and postpartum recovery tips.

Pregnancy Exercises

Walking

Walking improves blood circulation and keeps your body active.

Stretching

Gentle stretching reduces pain and improves flexibility.

Pelvic Floor

Strengthens pelvic muscles before and after childbirth.

Healthy Nutrition

  • 🥗 Eat fresh vegetables every day
  • 🍎 Eat fruits rich in vitamins
  • 🥚 Include protein in every meal
  • 🥛 Drink milk if recommended by your doctor
  • 💧 Drink enough water every day
  • 🥜 Eat healthy snacks like nuts
